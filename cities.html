Pilot cities

Three proposed zones, each inspired by a Harappan site.

Kutch water city

Inspired by Dholavira. A dry-region town built around rainwater harvesting, stepped tanks and shaded lanes.

Rakhigarhi core

Inspired by Rakhigarhi. A farmland-linked town centred on markets, workshops and a district granary for food security.

Lothal port hub

Inspired by Lothal. A coastal logistics and craft hub with a tidal dock and low-carbon freight.
