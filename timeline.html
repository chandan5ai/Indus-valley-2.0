Timeline

From the first Harappan towns to the first Indus Valley 2.0 pilot.

  1. c. 3300 BCE

    Early Harappan settlements appear.

  2. c. 2600 BCE

    Mature phase: planned cities with drains and standard bricks.

  3. c. 1900 BCE

    Gradual decline and shift of settlements.

  4. Phase 1

    Research, site surveys and open design standards.

  5. Phase 2

    First pilot neighbourhood built and monitored.

  6. Phase 3

    Scale to three pilot zones and publish results.
