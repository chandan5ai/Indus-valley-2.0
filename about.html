About

What Indus Valley 2.0 is, and why it looks back to move forward.

Indus Valley 2.0 is a concept for new towns that borrow proven ideas from the Indus Valley Civilization (c. 2600 to 1900 BCE) and combine them with modern materials, sensors and clean energy.
