Vision

Three principles guide every design decision.

Water first

Every street plan starts from where rain falls, where it is stored and where it drains.

Walk first

Daily needs within a short walk: school, market, clinic, work.

Repair first

Standard modules and local materials so buildings can be fixed, not replaced.

Our goal

Prove the model in three pilot zones, publish the open design standards, and help other regions adopt them.
